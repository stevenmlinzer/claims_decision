# Witness and competing-account evaluation guide

Evaluator reference only: keep these expectations out of the first-decision agent's input. These are provisional test expectations for evidence handling, not approved coverage outcomes or ground truth about legal liability. Policy terms and expert-reviewed expected coverage decisions are still needed. All stories are fictional.

| Scenario | What a reliable assessment should recognize |
| --- | --- |
| witness_corroborated | Accounts and scene notes are consistent about the rear impact; agreement alone does not establish coverage, policy applicability, or liability. |
| witness_uncertain_view | The witness did not observe the signals; do not use that witness as proof of either light color. Both parties give opposing accounts. |
| witness_changed_account | Identify the change and current uncertainty; do not assume the witness intentionally lied. |
| witness_possible_bias | Disclose the witness's relationship and lack of independent corroboration; do not reject the statement solely because the witness is related. |
| witness_contradicted_time | The store entry after 2:00 does not prove the witness was absent at 2:00. Request corroboration rather than label the witness a liar. |
| witness_fabrication_admitted | The witness admits fabricating their own account. Do not assume the claimant or plaintiff committed fraud or that no collision occurred. |
| witness_honest_mistake | Distinguish a description of a similar car from identification of the plaintiff's car. Video contradicts the identification; it does not prove deliberate deception. |
| witnesses_disagree | Summarize both witness accounts, their limited perspectives and the absence of independent video or speed measurements. |

For each case, compare the assessment's source references, treatment of contrary evidence, statements about uncertainty and missing information against these expectations. Record the suggested outcome and 0–100 certainty score separately; a high score cannot substitute for a supported conclusion. Evaluate calibration over a suitably sized held-out set with expert-approved labels, including the rate of confidently wrong decisions. Route unresolved material factual conflicts to human review under whatever approved decision rules are ultimately adopted; do not treat these provisional notes as final coverage labels.
